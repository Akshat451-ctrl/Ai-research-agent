# Orbitra Mobility — Internal Strategy Brief (FY2026)

> FICTIONAL SAMPLE DOCUMENT. Orbitra Mobility is an invented company created
> to test document retrieval. None of these figures describe a real business.

## 1. Company snapshot

Orbitra Mobility builds electric scooters for last-mile delivery fleets in
Pune, Indore and Coimbatore. The company was founded in 2022 and had 214
full-time employees at the end of FY2025. Headquarters are in Pune.

Orbitra does not sell to individual consumers. Every customer is a business
that operates a delivery fleet, such as grocery, pharmacy and food delivery
companies.

## 2. Fleet and pricing

At the end of FY2025 Orbitra had 18,600 scooters deployed across 41 fleet
customers. Scooters are leased, not sold. The standard lease is 3,450 rupees
per scooter per month, which includes maintenance and battery swaps.

Customers with more than 1,000 scooters receive a volume discount of 12
percent on the standard lease price. Seven of the 41 customers qualify for
this discount, and together they operate 11,200 of the 18,600 scooters.

## 3. Battery supply

Orbitra's battery packs come from a single supplier, Veltrax Cells, under a
contract that expires in March 2027. Each pack costs 38,000 rupees and lasts
roughly 1,400 charge cycles. Battery packs are the largest single component
cost, making up about 44 percent of the cost of each scooter.

The leadership team considers the single-supplier arrangement the company's
most serious operational risk. A second supplier is being evaluated, but no
contract has been signed.

## 4. Customer retention

Monthly fleet churn — the share of leased scooters returned and not
replaced — averaged 1.8 percent in FY2025. The target for FY2026 is 1.2
percent. Most returned scooters came from customers with fewer than 200
scooters, who cited the lack of a service centre in their city.

## 5. FY2026 plan

The FY2026 plan has three priorities:

1. Grow the deployed fleet from 18,600 to 27,000 scooters.
2. Open service centres in Nagpur and Madurai to reduce churn among smaller
   fleet customers.
3. Sign a second battery supplier before the Veltrax contract renewal
   negotiations begin in October 2026.

Capital expenditure for FY2026 is budgeted at 96 crore rupees, of which 71
crore is allocated to new scooters and 14 crore to the two service centres.

## 6. Key risks

- Single battery supplier (see section 3).
- A proposed state-level change to commercial EV registration rules in
  Maharashtra could delay new deployments in Pune by up to two quarters.
- Two of the seven discount-tier customers have contracts ending in FY2026;
  together they lease 3,900 scooters.
